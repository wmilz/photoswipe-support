Privacy Policy

PhotoSwipe · Effective October 26, 2026

PhotoSwipe does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Your photos

PhotoSwipe asks for access to your photo library so you can review and delete photos. Your photos and videos never leave your device through PhotoSwipe and are never uploaded to any server. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple's Photos framework may download full-size originals from iCloud when you view them; that transfer is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Your review progress and settings

Which photos you've kept or reviewed, and your app settings, are saved only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, PhotoSwipe schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere.

No tracking or analytics

PhotoSwipe contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking code, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

PhotoSwipe does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on GitHub.
