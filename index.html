Common questions

Where do deleted photos go?
PhotoSwipe deletes photos through iOS, which asks you to confirm and then moves them to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app. You can recover them there for up to 30 days.

Can I accidentally delete a favorite?
No. Photos marked as favorites are protected from deletion.

PhotoSwipe can't see my photos.
Open the Settings app, go to Apps → PhotoSwipe → Photos, and choose Full Access. If you picked "Limited Access", PhotoSwipe only sees the photos you selected.

Some photos take a moment to load.
If you use iCloud Photos with "Optimize iPhone Storage", full-size originals are downloaded from iCloud when you view them.
